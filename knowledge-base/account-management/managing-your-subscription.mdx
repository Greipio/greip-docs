Use **Manage billing** on the [Subscription page](https://dashboard.greip.io/subscription) to update your payment method, view invoices, change your plan, or cancel.

Clicking **Manage billing** opens the Stripe billing portal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subscription page">
    Go to the [Subscription page](https://dashboard.greip.io/subscription).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Manage billing">
    Under **Current Subscription**, click **Manage billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Update billing details or cancel as needed. When finished, use the link in the portal to return to the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cancellation

When you cancel, your subscription stays active until the **end of the current billing period** (or until the trial ends, if you are still on a trial).

New accounts that cancel do **not** fall back to a free plan and cannot start another trial. Subscribe again from the dashboard to restore access. Existing grandfathered free accounts keep their free quota if they cancel a paid plan.

For Greip's cancellation terms, see the [Cancellation Policy](https://greip.io/legal-and-security/cancellation-policy).

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, open **Manage billing** and update your payment method so the next charge can go through.

## Before deleting your account

Cancel your subscription first via **Manage billing**. See [Account deletion](/knowledge-base/account-management/account-deletion).
