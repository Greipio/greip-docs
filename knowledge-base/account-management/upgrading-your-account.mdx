Standard and Premium plans renew automatically each billing period.

## Subscribe or renew

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subscription page">
    Go to the [Subscription page](https://dashboard.greip.io/subscription) from the account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Upgrade">
    Under **Current Subscription**, click **Upgrade**.
    <Frame caption="Greip Dashboard — Upgrade">
      ![Greip Dashboard - Upgrading the account subscription](/images/dashboard-upgrade.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose plan and billing period">
    Select **Standard** or **Premium**, then **Monthly** or **Annual**, and click **Proceed to payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    You are redirected to a secure payment page. Choose the payment method you prefer. After payment, you return to the dashboard and your plan is activated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Promotion codes

On the payment page, use **Add promotion code** to apply a valid code before paying.

If you arrived via a referral link, a first-invoice discount may already be applied automatically — you cannot combine that with a manual promotion code on the same checkout.

### Auto-renewal

Your plan **renews automatically** at the end of each billing period.

Renewal invoices appear under **Invoices** on the Subscription page. See [Downloading invoices](/knowledge-base/account-management/downloading-invoices).

To update your payment method, view invoices, or cancel, use **Manage billing**. See [Managing your subscription](/knowledge-base/account-management/managing-your-subscription).

## Plans with an expiry date

If your Subscription page shows an expiry date, your access continues until that date. To renew, click **Upgrade** before it passes.

## Pay-as-you-go (PAYG)

PAYG accounts pay per invoice. Use **Pay now** on the Subscription page for any unpaid invoice. See [Downloading invoices](/knowledge-base/account-management/downloading-invoices).
