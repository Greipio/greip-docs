Invoices for your account are listed on the [Subscription page](https://dashboard.greip.io/subscription) under **Invoices**.

## Subscription invoices (**View invoice**)

Recurring subscription charges (including renewals) open in your browser as a hosted invoice or PDF.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subscription page">
    Go to the [Subscription page](https://dashboard.greip.io/subscription).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click View invoice">
    In the **Invoices** table, find a paid row and click **View invoice**.
    <Frame caption="Greip Dashboard — Invoices">
      ![Greip Dashboard - Downloading the invoices](/images/download-invoices.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or print">
    A new tab opens with the invoice. You can print or save it from there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also find past subscription invoices under **Manage billing**. See [Managing your subscription](/knowledge-base/account-management/managing-your-subscription).

## Older and PAYG invoices (**Download**)

Some one-time payments and **pay-as-you-go (PAYG)** invoices can be downloaded as PDFs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subscription page">
    Go to the [Subscription page](https://dashboard.greip.io/subscription).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Download">
    For these invoices, the action is labeled **Download**. The PDF saves to your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Unpaid PAYG invoices

If an invoice is unpaid, the row shows **Pay now** instead of **Download** or **View invoice**. Click **Pay now** to complete payment for that invoice.
